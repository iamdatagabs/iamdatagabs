SPECIALIZATION / DATA + CLINICAL CONTEXT

Data Health
Engineering.

Data engineering applied to healthcare: building the infrastructure that turns clinical, laboratory, physiological, and imaging data into interoperable, reliable information that care teams and researchers can use.

WHAT DOES A DATA HEALTH ENGINEER DO?

A Data Health Engineer designs and maintains systems that collect, integrate, validate, organize, and deliver health data. The role sits at the intersection of data engineering, health informatics, software, and clinical teams — ensuring that meaning, quality, security, context, and traceability are preserved throughout the data lifecycle.

01 / DATA PLATFORM

Data engineering

ETL/ELT, batch and streaming pipelines, modeling, SQL/NoSQL databases, data quality, lineage, observability, and APIs.

02 / INTEROPERABILITY

Clinical interoperability

FHIR, HL7 v2, DICOM, and terminologies such as LOINC, SNOMED CT, ICD-10, and common data model concepts.

03 / CLINICAL CONTEXT

Health informatics

Understanding electronic health records, tests, signals, clinical events, care pathways, and data semantics.

04 / COMPUTATIONAL HEALTH

AI & applied computing

Reproducible datasets, ML preparation, medical imaging, time series, physiological simulation, and decision support.
